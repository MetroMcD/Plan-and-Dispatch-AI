# Contributing

*[Deutsche Fassung](CONTRIBUTING.md)*

Short and honest: **I do not accept code contributions.**

Plan and Dispatch AI is a side project by a private individual. The source code
is not public, and the licence permits neither forks nor derivative works. This
is no reflection on your work — I simply cannot maintain third-party code or the
variants that would grow out of it.

## What helps

- **Bug reports.** As an issue in this repository, please. Useful details: the
  version (Admin → About this app), what you did, what happened, what you
  expected, and — if available — the output of
  `docker compose logs dispatcher --tail 50`.
- **Questions** about installation, integrations or operation.
- **Feature requests.** I will give you a straight yes or no rather than leaving
  things open.

## What does not help

Pull requests, patches or suggestions in source form. I will not look at them,
so that no question of authorship or licensing can arise.

## Security issues

Please do **not** open a public issue. Write to info@dispatcher-ai.de instead.
I will get back to you once I have assessed the report.
